# The Company That Owns a Piece of Everything You Buy (BlackRock)

7 escenas × 8 s, 9:16, sin voces ni música. Sin logos (el nombre se pone en CapCut si se quiere).

1. Your phone, your coffee, your burger, your shoes… they all have something in common.
2. A company most people have never heard of owns a piece of almost all of them.
3. It's called BlackRock. And it manages more than ten trillion dollars.
4. It's among the biggest shareholders of companies like Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Coca-Cola, McDonald's…
5. When you buy almost anything… a tiny part of that money ends up connected to them.
6. And because they own a piece of almost every company… they get a vote in almost all of them.
7. You've probably never heard their name. But you pay them every single day. So next time you buy something… ask yourself: who really owns it?

Matices: BlackRock gestiona (no posee) el dinero de sus clientes; suele estar entre los 3 mayores accionistas
(con Vanguard y State Street) con participaciones pequeñas (~5–8 %); vota en juntas, cada vez más delegando en clientes.
