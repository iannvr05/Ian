# Your money is worthless (and it all started with a shell)

7 escenas × 8 s, 9:16, sin voces ni música. Guion de Ian (inglés).

| # | Narration |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your money is worthless. And it all started with a shell. |
| 2 | 3,000 years ago in China, if you wanted shoes… you paid with a pig. And the shoemaker hated pigs. |
| 3 | Until a ship brought something nobody had ever seen. Shells. |
| 4 | They sparkled. They couldn't be faked. And they were completely useless. |
| 5 | And yet… everyone wanted them. Why? Because everyone wanted them. |
| 6 | Today is the same, you don't pay with shells. You pay with paper. With numbers on a screen. |
| 7 | That are worth something… only because we all believe they are. |

Notas: las conchas de cauri se usaron como moneda en la China de la dinastía Shang (h. 1600–1046 a. C.); llegaban
por comercio desde el océano Índico (lo del barco es licencia narrativa). Más tarde se hicieron imitaciones en
hueso y bronce, así que "no se podían falsificar" es una simplificación.
